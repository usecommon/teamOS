Borrow these

  • The EXTRACTED / INFERRED / AMBIGUOUS edge tag — sharper than our fuzzy confidence:"medium". Fold it into the L3 lineage schema and the provenance-peek viz.
  • Lead with "no model in the trust path" — they sell it as privacy; we treat it as an internal invariant. Say it out loud.
  • Their SKILL.md + hook install as a reference kit for FK6c packaging.
  • An honest efficiency metric (their "71.5× token reduction") alongside our thesis gates.
  • "Surprises / questions" report framing for the Day One Brief and FK8 discovery.

Where we can't be copied

  • Everything green. Graphify has no gate, envelope, breaker, deletion, or per-principal encryption — because it never acts or holds others' data.
  • The graph-view lesson: their force-directed graph.html earns its keep only because it's an interface to a traversable graph.json. A graph is UI, not decoration.
  • Initiative under gates, on the record is the product Graphify structurally can't become without building our whole governance stack.
