The envelope · touchpoint #7

The autonomy you've earned

Your agent acts on its own inside a boundary you set and it earns. The boundary moves on evidence: a run of accepted work widens it one notch; a single rejection above a severity line snaps it back. It climbs slowly and falls at once — trust in a machine should be hard to win and easy to lose.
