# Is "agent control plane / mission control" a defined market? (2026-07-12)

**Verdict:** A **newly named, actively-forming** category — recognized by every major analyst house by name, with early sizing on the table, but **no Magic Quadrant or Forrester Wave yet.** Crossed from "vendors coining terms" to "analysts naming and initiating coverage" in Q4 2025. Sits at Gartner's Peak of Inflated Expectations. You would enter *during* category formation.

## Category is named (multiply, un-consolidated)
- **Gartner: "AI Agent Management Platform (AMP)"** — "the centralized control layer for heterogeneous enterprise agent ecosystems" (Oct 2025 note: *"The Most Valuable Real Estate in AI"*). Six pillars: security, libraries, tooling, dashboard, marketplace, observability. Explicitly: "A dashboard… is not an AMP." Gartner's **first Hype Cycle for Agentic AI (2026)** breaks out agent-management platforms as a distinct profile. ([xpander/Gartner](https://xpander.ai/resources/ai-agent-management-platforms-gartner), [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/hype-cycle-for-agentic-ai))
- **Forrester: "agent control plane"** — coined Dec 2025; "inventories, governs, orchestrates, and assures heterogeneous AI agents across vendors." **Initiating research; not yet a Wave, no vendors scored.** ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/announcing-our-evaluation-of-the-agent-control-plane-market/))
- **"AgentOps"** — practitioner/vendor term (IBM, AgentOps.ai). **VCs** say "agent orchestration/operations."
- **No MQ or Wave exists as of mid-2026.**

## Three-plane model (Forrester) — validates the build/watch/control split
**Build plane** (frameworks, model access) → **orchestration plane** (embed agents in workflows) → **control plane**, which "sits outside both" as out-of-band governance. Community framing: *"observability tells you what happened; a control plane governs what can happen."* Control is recognized as **distinct** from observability and orchestration — the freshest development (late 2025). ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/agent-control-planes-still-need-a-robust-standards-stack/), [Activant](https://www.activantcapital.com/research/the-agent-control-plane))

## Sizing (dispersed — the tell of a pre-consensus category)
- AI orchestration: $11.0B (2025) → $30.2B (2030), 22.3% CAGR ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/PressReleases/ai-orchestration.asp))
- Agentic orchestration & memory: $6.27B → $28.45B (2030), 35.3% CAGR; observability sub-segment fastest at ~37.5% ([Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/agentic-artificial-intelligence-orchestration-and-memory-systems-market))
- "AgentOps infrastructure": $1.8B (2025) → $58.4B (2034), 45% CAGR ([MarketIntelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/agentops-ai-infrastructure-platform-market))
- **Gartner's bet: AMP spend $15B by 2029 vs <$5M today** — a near-vertical curve = category being born.
- A discrete "governance/control" number barely exists — still folded into AMP/orchestration totals. That absence is a signal.

## Investment: talking control, funding observability

## Enterprise vs solo
**Almost entirely enterprise/IT-governance** — every analyst frame is the enterprise buyer governing multi-vendor fleets. **"There is no analyst-recognized individual/solo control-plane category. That whitespace is unclaimed in the research."**

## Maturity + implication
Emerging/mid-formation, born Q4 2025, Peak of Inflated Expectations, shakeout warned. Entering now = early enough to shape vocabulary and be a defining vendor before the first Wave/MQ (12–24mo out). **Defensible move: own the term for the segment analysts have NOT claimed — the individual/solo control plane** — rather than fight named giants in the enterprise AMP lane.
